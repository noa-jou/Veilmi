# Join the Veilmi Closed Test

Veilmi is currently available through a **Google Play Closed Test**.

Before I can apply for a public Production release, Google requires at least
**12 testers** to remain opted into the Closed Test for at least **14 continuous days**.

If you have an Android device, you are very welcome to help test Veilmi.

You do **not** need programming, cybersecurity, or cryptography knowledge.

In fact, feedback from ordinary users is especially useful because I already know
how the app is supposed to work. If something is confusing, unclear, awkward,
or behaves differently from what you expected, I would genuinely like to know.

---

## Before You Start

Please use the **same Google account** for:

- joining the Veilmi Testers Google Group;
- joining the Google Play Closed Test;
- accessing Google Play on your Android device.

Using different Google accounts may prevent Google Play from recognizing your
testing access.

---

## Step 1 — Join the Veilmi Testers Google Group

Sign in to the Google account you use with Google Play, then open:

https://groups.google.com/g/veilmi-testers

Click:

**Join the group**

Joining this Google Group makes your Google account eligible to access the
Veilmi Closed Test.

---

## Step 2 — Join the Google Play Closed Test

After joining the Google Group, open:

https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.veilmi.app

Click:

**Become a tester**

This opts your Google account into the Veilmi Closed Test.

---

## Step 3 — Install Veilmi

After joining the test, Google Play should allow you to install Veilmi normally.

If needed, open:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.veilmi.app

and install Veilmi on your Android device.

---

## Step 4 — Please Stay in the Test for at Least 14 Days

This part is important.

Please remain opted into the Veilmi Closed Test for at least **14 continuous days**.

You do **not** need to use Veilmi every day.

Simply remaining in the test already helps me satisfy the Google Play testing
requirement.

However, if you have time to try the app during those two weeks, I would really
appreciate your feedback.

---

## What Can You Test?

You can try things such as:

- encrypting and decrypting a normal message;
- copying encrypted text into another app and back into Veilmi;
- trying longer messages;
- trying emoji or unusual characters;
- trying different languages;
- trying different protection levels;
- checking whether buttons and instructions are easy to understand;
- noticing whether anything behaves differently from what you expected.

You do not need to test everything.

Even one small observation can be useful.

---

## If Something Does Not Work

If Google Groups says that you cannot join the Veilmi Testers group, try
**reloading the page first**.

We have seen this happen before, and simply refreshing the page fixed it.

If Google Play suddenly behaves as if Veilmi does not exist, do not panic.

Try:

1. closing the Play Store completely;
2. turning off your screen for a short while;
3. opening the testing link again later.

This may be caused by a temporary Google Play / Google account permission
synchronization or cache issue.

Also make sure that the Google account signed in to Google Play is the **same
account** you used to join the Google Group and the Closed Test.

If it still does not work, please send me a message or screenshot and I will try
to help.

## Security Notice

Veilmi has **not undergone an independent professional security audit**.

It is an open-source learning project that I have tried to build and document
carefully, but I do not present it as a formally audited security product.

Please do not use Veilmi for extremely sensitive information during testing.

The source code is available here:

https://github.com/noa-jou/Veilmi

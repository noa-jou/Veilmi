# Veilmi Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 11, 2026**

Veilmi is designed to encrypt and decrypt text locally on the user's device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Veilmi handles user information.

## Local Message Processing

Messages and shared passphrases entered into Veilmi are processed locally on
the user's device.

Veilmi does not send message content or shared passphrases to a Veilmi-operated
server.

Veilmi does not require a user account.

## Information Stored on the Device

Veilmi stores a small amount of application preference data locally, including:

- the selected interface language;
- the selected protection level.

These preferences are stored on the device using local application storage.

Veilmi does not intentionally save message contents or shared passphrases to
its own persistent storage.

Device Check benchmark results are used locally to help suggest a practical
protection level for the device.

## Clipboard

When the user chooses to copy a message, Veilmi places that text on the
device's system clipboard.

The clipboard is managed by the operating system. Its retention and
accessibility may depend on the device, Android version, keyboard, and other
installed software.

Users should avoid leaving sensitive plaintext on the clipboard longer than
necessary.

## Network and Tracking

Veilmi's encryption and decryption operations do not require a network
connection.

Veilmi does not currently include advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking,
or a developer-operated service that receives users' messages or passphrases.

## External Links

Veilmi may provide links to external websites, such as:

- the Veilmi GitHub repository;
- project documentation;
- Buy Me a Coffee.

Opening these links may launch a web browser or another application.

These external services operate independently from Veilmi and are governed by
their own privacy policies and data practices.

## Data Sharing

Veilmi does not sell user data.

Veilmi does not intentionally transmit message contents or shared passphrases
to the developer or to third parties.

Information that users voluntarily place into another application — for
example, by pasting an encrypted Veilmi message into a messaging service — is
then handled according to that service's own policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Application preferences remain on the device until they are changed, the app's
data is cleared, or Veilmi is uninstalled.

Message contents and shared passphrases are not intentionally retained by
Veilmi in persistent application storage.

Data copied to the system clipboard is controlled by the device operating
system rather than Veilmi.

## Security Limitations

Veilmi is intended to protect message content before it is sent through another
communication channel.

It does not hide information such as who is communicating with whom, when
messages are sent, or how often people communicate.

Veilmi also cannot protect information from a compromised device, malicious
keyboard, screen capture, weak shared passphrase, or a recipient who reveals
the decrypted message.

## Children's Privacy

Veilmi is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Because Veilmi does not provide user accounts or a developer-operated message
service, it does not intentionally collect children's personal information.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Veilmi's features or data-handling
practices change.

The current version will be published at the same public Privacy Policy
location.
